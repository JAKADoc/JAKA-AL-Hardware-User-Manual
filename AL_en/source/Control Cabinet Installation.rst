.. _Installation-Environment:

Installation Environment
---------------------------

CAB V3 needs to be installed in a dry place with good ventilation and cooled by natural convection. The following figure presents the minimum distances and minimum clearances of control cabinet installation.

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/CAB_L、CAB_H最小安装空间.*
   :width: 13.24cm
   :align: center

   Minimum clearances of CAB L and CAB H

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/CAB_LiteAC、CAB_LiteDC最小安装空间.*
   :width: 13.55cm
   :align: center

   Minimum clearances of CAB LiteAC and CAB LiteDC

.. attention::

   **Inoperable equipment/reduction of lifetime:**

   - Ensure sufficient airflow for the control cabinet, especially if the device is installed in an enclosure.
   - Do not install in damp or water-splashed environment.
   - Keep away from heat sources such as stoves.
   - Do not use this product near corrosive and flammable gases such as hydrogen sulfide, chlorine, ammonia, sulfur, chlorinated gases, acid, alkali, salt, combustible, etc.
   - Use CAB V3 below an altitude of 2000 m (6562 ft), and please derate if the altitude is above 2000 m (6562 ft).

   Failure to follow these instructions can result in equipment damage.

.. _Install-CAB-L-and-CAB-H:

Install CAB L and CAB H
=======================

Control cabinet should be mounted securely before using it. There are three mounting methods available for CAB L and CAB H. Refer to :ref:`Technical Data <Technical-Data>` for their weight.

.. _Support-Foot-Mounting:

Support Foot Mounting
---------------------

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/地脚安装示意.*
   :width: 6.48cm
   :align: center

   Support foot mounting illustration

The mounting steps are as follows:

.. list-table:: Support foot mounting steps
   :widths: 7 45 48
   :header-rows: 1

   * -
     - **Steps**
     - **Tools**

   * - 1
     - According to the dimension of support feet, drill four holes on the mounting plane.
     - Aperture: 8.5 mm (0.33 in)

       If the mounting plate is steel, the thread depth must be at least 8 mm (0.3 in); if it is aluminum, ensure a minimum thread depth of 16 mm (0.6 in).

   * - 2
     - Align the holes of the support feet and mounting plane, and secure the control cabinet by screws.
     - Screw type: M8

       Screw quantity: 4

       Tightening torque: 23 Nm (203.6 Ibf·in)

   * - 3
     - Ensure that the mounting surface is smooth and free of vibration and confirm that the control cabinet is securely mounted.
     - When the mounting plane is mobile, to reduce the impact of vibration on CAB V3 during movement, it is recommended to add rubber gaskets between CAB V3 and the mounting plane.

.. _Rear-Mounting:

Rear Mounting
-------------

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/背面安装示意.*
   :width: 9.52cm
   :align: center

   Rear mounting illustration

The mounting steps are as follows:

.. list-table:: Rear mounting steps
   :widths: 7 44 49
   :header-rows: 1

   * -
     - **Steps**
     - **Tools**

   * - 1
     - Loosen and remove the four knurled thumb screws on the rear panel of the control cabinet.
     - /

   * - 2
     - According to the control cabinet's dimensions, drill four holes on the mounting plane.
     - Aperture: 6.6 mm (0.26 in)

   * - 3
     - Align the holes of the control cabinet back and mounting plane, and secure the control cabinet by screws.
     - Screw type: M6

       Screw quantity: 4

       Tightening torque: 1.2 Nm (132.8 Ibf·in)

   * - 4
     - Ensure that the mounting surface is smooth and free of vibration and confirm that the control cabinet is securely mounted.
     - When the mounting plane is mobile, to reduce the impact of vibration on CAB V3 during movement, it is recommended to add rubber gaskets between CAB V3 and the mounting plane.

.. _Bracket-Mounting:

Bracket Mounting
-------------------

CAB LiteDC and CAB LiteAC can be mounted through bracket mounting.

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/挂耳安装示意.*
   :width: 12.49cm
   :align: center

   Bracket mounting illustration

The mounting steps are as follows:

.. list-table:: Bracket mounting steps
   :widths: 7 44 49
   :header-rows: 1

   * -
     - **Steps**
     - **Tools**

   * - 1
     - According to the control cabinet's dimensions, drill four holes on the mounting plane.
     - Aperture: 3.4 mm (0.13 in)

       If the mounting plate is steel, the thread depth must be at least 3 mm (0.1 in); if it is aluminum, ensure a minimum thread depth of 6 mm (0.2 in).

   * - 2
     - Align the holes of the brackets and mounting plane, and secure the two brackets by screws.
     - Screw type: M3

       Screw quantity: 4

       Tightening torque: 1.2 Nm (10.62 Ibf·in)

   * - 3
     - Ensure that the mounting surface is smooth and free of vibration and confirm that the control cabinet is securely mounted.
     - When the mounting plane is mobile, to reduce the impact of vibration on CAB V3 during movement, it is recommended to add rubber gaskets between CAB V3 and the mounting plane.

.. _DIN-Rail-Mounting:

DIN Rail Mounting
------------------

CAB LiteDC and CAB LiteAC can be mounted through DIN rail mounting.

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/安装导轨示意0.*
   :width: 9.46cm
   :align: center

.. figure:: ../../images/07_运输及调试/安装导轨示意.*
   :width: 9.46cm
   :align: center

   DIN rail mounting illustration

The mounting steps are as follows:

.. list-table:: DIN rail mounting steps
   :widths: 7 44 49
   :header-rows: 1

   * -
     - **Steps**
     - **Tools**

   * - 1
     - Align the mounting holes on the rail clamps with the eight mounting holes on the rear panel of the control cabinet, then use screws to secure them together.
     - Screw type: M3

       Screw quantity: 8

       Tightening torque: 1.2 Nm (10.62 Ibf·in)

   * - 2
     - Mount the control cabinet with pre-installed rail mounting accessories onto the DIN rail.
     - Width of rail: 35 mm (1.4 in)
