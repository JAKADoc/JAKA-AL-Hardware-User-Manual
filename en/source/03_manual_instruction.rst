Manual Instruction
******************

.. _About the Manual:

About the Manual
++++++++++++++++

The manual contains:

- Precautions for the use of JAKA |product_name| robots.
- Installation and commissioning of JAKA |product_name| robots.
- Cleaning and maintenance of JAKA |product_name| robots.

.. _Manual Reader:

Manual Reader
+++++++++++++

The manual is intended for:

- Operators.
- Commissioning staff.
- Maintenance staff.
- Integrators.

.. _References:

References
++++++++++

Documentation referred to in the manual:

- JAKA |jaka_app| Software User Manual.
- JAKA Service Manual.
- JAKA Touch I User Manual.
- JAKA Touch II User Manual.

.. note::

   The documents above are available from the JAKA website at \ `www.jaka.com <http://www.jaka.com>`__\ .

.. _Prerequisites:

Prerequisites
+++++++++++++

The reader should:

- Be trained and certified by JAKA and have the required knowledge of mechanical and electrical installation/repair/maintenance work.
- Be trained to respond to emergencies or abnormal situations.
